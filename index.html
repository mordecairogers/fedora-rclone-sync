Privacy policy

Personal use · Local-first files

Your notes, on your computers.

Fedora Rclone Sync is a personal configuration of rclone for synchronizing selected Obsidian folders between the owner's Fedora computers and their Google Drive account.

What this setup is for

The intended workflow keeps complete local copies of selected folders so notes can be edited offline. Reviewed synchronization jobs exchange changes with Google Drive, while independent local backups help preserve earlier versions.

This is the owner's personal setup. It is not a public file service and does not offer account registration or a sign-in form on this website.

Why Google Drive access is requested

Access is used to list folders, read and download files, and, when synchronization is explicitly enabled by the owner, upload, update, rename or delete files in the selected folders.

The Google Drive permission requested by rclone allows access across the account's Drive files, excluding Google's Application Data Folder. The selection of folders is enforced by the local synchronization configuration; the Google permission itself is broader.

Where your data goes

The rclone program runs on the owner's computers and communicates directly with Google. This informational website does not receive Drive files, OAuth credentials or authorization tokens. It contains no embedded Google login, analytics scripts or file-upload form.
